
This is my first game with python! It's a little text game you play in the terminal. I made it to practice what I've been learning (loops, if/else, functions and stuff). IT has been made as a project for folktale from hackclub.

The game is about a stranger  that asks you to help solve some riddles that his dad left behind before he died. There's a book with 25 entries but only one of them is the real final riddle, the other ones are just tricks. You solve 5 tasks, and each one gives you a number. Then you do some math with the numbers to find out which entry to look for in the book.

You play this way:

1. Type your name.
2. Say yes or no when he asks for a favor (if you say no he keeps asking).
3. Pick a task from 1 to 5, in any order you want.
4. Solve the riddle. If you get it wrong you get one more try or you go to choosing a task again.
5. When you're done pick 6 and type the final answer.

You need Python installed. Then open a terminal and type:

game.py

Write down the number from every task! You have to add them all together and then divide by 4 as is said in the introduction, but you will probably forget so have it in mind. That's the final answer.
